About Datra Analytics

India's leading real estate data intelligence platform, transforming how you understand property markets

Democratizing Real Estate Data

At Datra Analytics, we believe that access to accurate, transparent real estate data should not be a privilege. Our platform aggregates millions of data points from government records, property registrations, and market transactions to deliver actionable intelligence that empowers buyers, sellers, investors, and developers to make informed decisions with confidence.

Government Records

Direct access to verified government property records, registration data, and official land documents across Indian states.

Property Data

Aggregated property registration analytics including sale deed trends, transaction value patterns, and historical records — fully privacy-compliant with no personal or sensitive buyer-seller data shared.

Market Analytics

In-depth market analysis with price trends, demand-supply metrics, and locality-level insights to understand market dynamics.

Data Intelligence

AI-powered insights and predictive analytics that transform raw data into strategic intelligence for smarter real estate decisions.
